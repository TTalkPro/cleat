This directory is the R6RS test suite from Racket (formerly PLT Scheme):
https://github.com/racket/r6rs, directory r6rs-test/tests/r6rs.
It reached cleat through IronScheme and skeg (https://github.com/IronScheme/IronScheme, skeg commits e02260a and 6cd25cc,
which adapt it to call-with-return and to try/finally in place of call/cc and dynamic-wind).
